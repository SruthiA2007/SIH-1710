## Problem Statement

Railway stations are complex environments with multiple platforms, ticket counters, waiting halls, food courts, restrooms, parking areas, and other facilities. Passengers, especially first-time visitors, senior citizens, tourists, and differently-abled individuals, often face challenges in locating these facilities efficiently. Traditional signboards and announcements are often insufficient in crowded stations, leading to confusion, delays, missed trains, and an overall poor travel experience.

With the increasing number of railway passengers, there is a growing need for a smart and intelligent navigation system that can provide real-time guidance within station premises. Such a system should help passengers quickly locate facilities, receive accurate directions, and access information through user-friendly digital platforms.

## Proposed Solution

The proposed solution is a Smart Railway Station Navigation System, a multi-platform navigation solution that assists passengers in finding facilities and destinations inside railway stations. The system will be accessible through a mobile application and digital kiosks installed at strategic locations within the station.

The mobile application will feature interactive 3D station maps, real-time navigation, route planning, and step-by-step directions to platforms and station facilities. Digital kiosks will provide similar navigation support for passengers who do not have access to smartphones. The system will also include voice-guided navigation and accessibility-friendly routes for visually impaired and physically challenged users.
